I am a second-year PhD student in the [Department of Statistics & Data Science](https://statistics.ucla.edu/) at [UCLA](https://www.ucla.edu/). I earned my MS in Applied and Computational Mathematics from the University of Washington and my BS in Data Theory from UCLA. I study deep learning, with the goal of developing a program for understanding feature learning in neural networks. Please see my [research page](https://ethanjyoung.github.io/research/) for more details. If you are an undergraduate student interested in working with me, please go to [this page](https://ethanjyoung.github.io/people/) first for some things to consider.
