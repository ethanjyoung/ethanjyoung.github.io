_Joint with Rishi Sonthalia and Guido Montúfar._

We prove a conditional equivalence theorem for two random matrix ensembles, covering weighted traces and bilinear forms of their Gram resolvents. The result permits general distributions for fixed-width column blocks in both ensembles.
