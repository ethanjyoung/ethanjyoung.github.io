_Joint with Mason A. Porter._

We study a notion of edge importance called _dynamical importance_, which is based on the spectral information (i.e., dominant eigenpair) of the adjacency matrix. The behavior of coupled Kuramoto oscillators on networks can be understood in terms of dynamical importance. Here is our [paper]({{ base_path }}/files/Dynamical_importance_and_network_perturbations.pdf).
