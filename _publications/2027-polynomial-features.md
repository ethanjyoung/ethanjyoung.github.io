_Joint with Rishi Sonthalia and Guido Montúfar_.

We show how one gradient step in a two-layer network creates nonlinear feature structure. For a fixed Hermite expansion of the activation, we approximate the trained and untrained features by finite-rank polynomial perturbations of a common nonlinear bulk. We identify a threshold combining network normalization, step size, and input-spike strength that determines which polynomial degrees can contribute at the bulk scale.
